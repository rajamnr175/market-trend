How verdicts are assigned

  • Strong momentum: price is above the 50D and 200D averages, the 50D is above the 200D, and the 200D is rising. The stock also beats Nifty by 15+ pts over 3 months, has RSI ≥ 55 and sits within 12% of its 52-week high.
  • Uptrend: the same moving-average structure, with price no more than 6% below the 50D.
  • Downtrend: price is below the 200D, and the 200D is falling or sits under the 50D.
  • Avoid now: a downtrend plus at least 3 of these: RSI below 40, ADX ≥ 25 with sellers dominant, within 8% of the 52-week low, down more than 8% in a month, or more than 5% below the 50D.
  • Sideways / correcting: everything else, meaning mixed signals.

Composite score (0–100)

Trend (50 pts): price vs the 20/50/200D averages, 50D vs 200D, the slope of both averages, and higher highs and lows over the last 20 sessions against the prior 20. Momentum (50 pts): 3- and 6-month performance against Nifty, RSI(14), the MACD histogram's sign and direction, and ADX/DMI.
